# Name and Logo

The code of Carnine2 is free software under the
[GNU General Public License v3](LICENSE). The license covers the code. It
does not cover the name **CarNine** (also written Carnine or Carnine2) or its
logos: the logo at the top of the [README](README.md)
(`resources/images/carnine-logo.jpg`) and the boot logos in
`resources/images/` (`rund`, `land`, `sport`). They belong to the project
owner, [DerKleinePunk](https://github.com/DerKleinePunk).

## Fine without asking

- Passing on Carnine2 **unchanged**, as source or as the released packages
  and SD card images, under its name and with its logos.
- Writing or talking about CarNine: articles, videos, reviews, talks.
- Saying that your own product or add-on **works with** CarNine, as long as
  it does not look like part of the project or made by it.

## Ask first

- Publishing a **changed** version (a fork, a modified image, your own
  packages) under the name CarNine or with its logos.
- Using the name or a logo for your own product, service, shop or
  advertising.
- Changing a logo and using it as one of the project.

## Forks

Forks are welcome, the GPL allows them. Give your fork **its own name and
logo**, and say where it comes from, for example "based on Carnine2 by
DerKleinePunk" with a link to this repository. Before you pass the fork on,
replace the CarNine logos in it, the boot logo in the image included
(`resources/debos/raspbian.yaml` installs `rund.png` as the splash).

## Asking

Open an [issue](https://github.com/DerKleinePunk/carnine2/issues) and say
what you want to do with the name or the logo. The answer comes there.
